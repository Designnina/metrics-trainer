## Platform

web

## Users

English-speaking product, UX, design, delivery, business, technical and AI practitioners who need a compact way to learn and repeatedly recall professional vocabulary and metrics.

## Product Purpose

Metrics Trainer is an interactive learning tool built around a large deck of professional cards. It helps people recognise concepts, understand definitions and formulas, practise distinctions, and build durable recall through flip, swipe, answer, progress and spaced-repetition mechanics.

## Positioning

The product teaches by making the learner commit to an answer before revealing the explanation, then routes difficult cards back into practice instead of treating a one-time quiz score as mastery.

## Operating Context

Learners use the app in short focused sessions on phones, tablets and desktop browsers. The main loop is Learn → flip or swipe → mark known/review → return to weak cards; Test, Metrics and Progress support deeper study and review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the existing HashRouter routes, card data, five topic groups, flip/swipe interaction, answer recording, filtering, local progress and theme switching.
- The topic groups are Product, UX Research, Design, Delivery & Business, and Tech & AI.
- The card corpus is 300+ English training cards; the separate Test question bank is retained and must not be silently discarded during visual work.
- The interface must be responsive from 320px through large desktop widths with no horizontal overflow and usable touch targets.
- The visual redesign should be materially distinct from generic SaaS/dashboard UI and should not introduce backend requirements.

## Brand Commitments

Product name: Metrics Trainer. Visual direction requested by the user: highly contemporary, designerly, constructive and structural; strongly unlike generic SaaS or visual-learning platforms.

## Product Principles

1. Recall before recognition: the learner commits before the answer appears.
2. Every card should explain what to do with the concept, not only name it.
3. Difficulty should change the next session, not merely decorate a score.
4. Structure should reduce cognitive load while preserving intellectual density.
5. Product truth and working mechanics outrank visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, long English copy, touch interaction and responsive reflow across phone and desktop widths.
